Our story

Born from a 2 am study session

The Late Shift began when a group of students noticed that every campus coffee spot closed before the library did. So we wrote a plan, borrowed a corner of the student union, and opened our doors.

Today we are run by volunteers and part-time student staff. Every cup helps fund study-group snacks and late-night shuttle passes.

What we believe

  • Good coffee should be affordable on a student budget.
  • A table, a plug, and a warm drink make better work.
  • Everyone is welcome, whether you stay five minutes or five hours.
